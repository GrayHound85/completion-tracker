# Update 2.0.0 patch notes

The biggest update so far. Completion Tracker now has its own page on the main menu, and the contract broker tells you a lot more about what you have and have not done.

## Completion Tracker page

- New **Completion Tracker** button on the main menu, under Achievements.
- An overall completion percentage. It is weighted by how long things take, so finishing a heist on Death Sentence counts for far more than buying a pistol, and the achievements cannot swamp everything else.
- A row for each category, each with its own completion ring: heists, level and infamy, achievements, weapons, attachments, masks, melee and throwables, cosmetics, perk decks, safe house, trophies, and cash and coins.
- Click a category for the full numbers. Blue lines open a list of exactly what you are still missing, for example which heists you have not finished on Overkill, or which weapons you have not bought and what they cost.
- Achievements show the ones you are closest to unlocking.
- Cash and coins shows what you have, what you have earned and spent, and how much you still need to buy every weapon, attachment and safe house upgrade.
- Switch between counting only content from DLC you own and counting everything in the game.

## Contract broker

- New difficulty selector next to the heist list. Heists turn green if you have completed them on the chosen difficulty or a harder one. It goes from Normal up to Death Sentence, with a final step for Death Sentence with One Down.
- Skulls on each heist picture show the hardest difficulty you have completed it on.
- New "hide completed" tick box, to see only what is left on the chosen difficulty.

## Other additions

- A popup at the end of a heist when you complete it for the first time, or on a harder difficulty than ever before.
- Attachments that show as out of stock but that you already own, fitted to another weapon, now get the owned marker.
- New options page under Options > Mod Options > Completion Tracker. Every addition can be switched on or off there, and changes apply straight away.

## Fixes

- Contracts on Death Wish were never turning green on Crime.net. They do now.
- The Crime.net highlight can be switched off without restarting the game.

## Notes

- The mod still needs nothing but SuperBLT.
- Your existing settings are kept.
